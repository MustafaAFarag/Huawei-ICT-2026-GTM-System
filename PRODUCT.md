# Caravan

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js and Twenty UI; NestJS API and BullMQ worker; PostgreSQL/Prisma; unmodified Twenty CRM and OpenDesign; Better Auth. Node 24 and pnpm. Azure Container Apps for the December demo, Huawei only after qualification.

## Users
Non-technical founders, small B2B teams and agencies in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. They need to prepare and review outreach without assembling a personal automation stack.

## Product Purpose
Chat-first: the home page is a single composer where the user asks Nabil (the assistant, Strobi avatar) for work; Nabil gathers facts, proposes one governed action, and files the resulting deliverable in the workspace. Screens exist for detail and review.
Run outbound in one place through ready-made plays, a persistent assistant and branded assets. Success is an approved first campaign in 30 minutes, an approved asset PDF in five minutes, and no unapproved sends or spending over caps.

## Capabilities and Constraints
PRD v3 is authoritative. December includes P1–P4 and two bilingual asset templates. English is the interface default; Arabic content is first-class. Email is sent through a sender platform; LinkedIn and call steps are manual. Synthetic or consented demonstration data only. Billing is a labelled simulation. Workspace permissions, citations, suppression, approval invalidation, budget controls and durable jobs are mandatory.

## Brand Commitments
Use Twenty's component library and restrained CRM interface conventions. Product interaction and integration precede bespoke visual refinement.

## Evidence on Hand
PRD v3, archived v1/v2 contracts, ten sourced research reports and an earlier Python prototype. No verified response-rate lift, live pilot, native-reviewer acceptance rate or deployed cloud environment.

## Product Principles
- Start from a play and show the next action.
- Show estimates before spending and enforce caps on the server.
- Keep facts, hypotheses and unknowns distinct.
- Human approval authorizes a concrete version and scope.
- Keep workspace records, credentials, jobs and assistant context separate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus and actionable loading/empty/error/success states. Desktop, tablet and narrow web layouts. Arabic shaping and RTL exports; isolate email addresses, URLs and identifiers as LTR.
