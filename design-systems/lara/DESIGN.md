# Lara Advisory

## Intent
A clear, polished corporate brand for evidence review, client reporting and practical sales assets. The supplied plan is the direction; no screenshot was available for direct comparison.

## Color
Navy #14283f carries text and identity. Gold #a37b2d marks hierarchy and decorative rules. White carries documents. Gold must not be the sole signal for approval or failure.

## Typography
Use locally available Arial and Arabic system sans-serif. Large headings lead each sheet; small source dates and version labels remain legible. Do not invent font ownership.

## Composition
Strong margins, separated account sections, narrow source annotations and restrained layers. Keep the purpose visible. Charts derive only from supplied records.

## States
Drafts, approvals, holds and simulated execution require explicit labels. Approval and evidence decisions accompany the version being reviewed. No decorative status may imply execution.

## Arabic and mixed strings
Arabic documents use RTL and browser shaping. Emails, links, identifiers and dates are isolated LTR. User content uses automatic direction. Print through Chromium for shaped Arabic PDFs.

## Editing
Text remains editable in generated HTML. Local document edits are not approved campaign edits. Save edited HTML explicitly. Reapprove campaign copy in the main workflow.

## Accessibility
Native controls, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive sheets and QR quiet zones. Avoid automatic print dialogs, tiny annotations and instructions communicated only by color.

## Motion
No document animation. Interaction feedback stays visible alongside the attempted action.

## Integration status
Manifest/design/tokens follow the documented minimal OpenDesign package convention. The local template renderer uses these brand values in isolated jobs. OpenDesign daemon execution, its token-schema guard and release pinning remain unavailable; this is not a certified bundled OpenDesign design system.
