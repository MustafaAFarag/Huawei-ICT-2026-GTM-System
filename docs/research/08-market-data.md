# 08. Market data for the PRD (sizes, sprawl, skills gap, MENA, Arabic)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Method: web search plus page fetches only. No paid tools. Nothing sent.

## How to read the tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | I opened the page (or PDF) and saw the number on it. |
| **Unverified** | Seen only in a search snippet, in secondary coverage, or the page returned 403 or timed out. |

Extra flags used in the "Note" column:
- **Vendor**: the publisher sells products in this space (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, Clay, Instantly). Their surveys can be real but the framing is self-serving.
- **Pay-wall**: the publisher sells the report. The public page shows only a headline number.
- **Secondary**: I opened a news page that quotes the publisher. The publisher's own page was blocked (Gartner pages returned HTTP 403 for every URL I tried).

I did not repeat numbers from reports 01, 02 and 04 unless I found a better source. Where I upgraded an earlier number, I say so.

## 2. Tool sprawl and time use

### 2.1 Salesforce State of Sales, 7th edition (the best primary source I found)

I downloaded and read the PDF. This **upgrades** report 01, which had the 40% figure as Unverified.

- Publisher: Salesforce Research. Published Feb 2026. **Vendor** (Salesforce sells an all-in-one platform and pushes consolidation).
- Method: n = 4,050 sales professionals, 22 countries, anonymous survey, Aug-Sep 2025, third-party panelists. 30% of respondents (n = 1,215) work at firms of 21-200 employees. No Middle East country in the sample (closest: India, Singapore).
- URL: https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/reports/sales/salesforce-state-of-sales-report-2026.pdf

How to use: this is a clean, citable source for "reps sell 40% of the time" and "teams run about 8 tools". It also contains a trap for our thesis: Salesforce's own data says teams are already moving toward one platform (84% plan to consolidate). That supports "all-in-one", but it also means big incumbents are chasing the same idea.

## 10. What this means for the PRD

1. Use the Salesforce numbers (40% selling, 8 tools, 42% overwhelmed) as the main pain evidence. They are Verified and recent. Footnote that Salesforce sells a platform.
2. Do not build the proposal on a market-size figure. Use "growing, SMB segments growing faster" and name each publisher.
3. The skills-gap claim for the target user (non-technical SMB staff) is still a hypothesis. The best support is indirect (Zapier training gap, Salesforce overwhelm). Plan 5-10 interviews to get a direct number.
4. For MENA, lead with the government and regulator numbers (Monsha'at, UAE Ministry, CST, US Commercial Service). They are Verified and neutral. Avoid funding totals as a demand signal.
5. For Arabic, the safe line is the gap: 0.6% of websites versus roughly 400M speakers. Say plainly that we have no demand data for Arabic business tools.
