# 05. Huawei ICT Competition and Huawei Cloud fit

> **Partly superseded by [report 06](06-huawei-ict-2026-27-rules.md) (2026–27 rules).** "MindSpore/CANN/ModelArts mandatory" is out of date for our region; the 2026–27 rule is deployment on Huawei Cloud with at least one Huawei Cloud service, plus Huawei AI "in combination with other technologies".

Research date: 2026-10-12. Web research only. Label key: **Verified** = read on the cited page or document. **Unverified** = search-snippet only, third-party, inferred, or from an older season.

## 0. Bottom line (read this first)

1. **Season mix-up.** The 2025-2026 season (10th edition) already ended. Its Global Final was in Shenzhen on 2-5 June 2026. "Huawei ICT Competition 2026" for you is the **2026-2027 season**, which is just starting. Registration is open. A Latin America outlet (8 Oct 2026) reports a 31 Dec 2026 deadline. That is not an Egypt source. **Verified**: [radioagricultura.cl](https://www.radioagricultura.cl/tendencias/viaja-a-china-gracias-a-tus-conocimientos-en-tecnologia-con-huawei_20261008/).
2. **I could not get the official 2026-2027 Innovation rulebook.** Huawei's own PDFs and Talent portal return HTTP 403 to automated fetches. The most detailed official Innovation document I could read is the **2023-2024 (8th edition)** one. It has the full scoring rubric, deliverables and team rules. Treat it as the best available guide, and ask your Huawei Talent Manager or ICT Academy contact to confirm for 2026-2027.
3. **"All Azure until we pass stage 1" is the weakest point of the PRD.** In every official Innovation document I found, using Huawei AI technology is mandatory and is explicitly scored. It is also cheaper to get Huawei resources than the PRD assumes. Details are in section 5.
4. **Your biggest scoring risk is not cloud. It is "Creativity" (30 points) and "Social value" (15 points).** A GTM and CRM assistant is a crowded category, and past winners are social-impact projects. Details are in sections 3 and 7.

Non-students or a mixed-university team probably cannot compete. If your team has a non-student founder, decide now who is on the roster. Whether an institution must be a Huawei ICT Academy to enter Innovation is **Unverified**. One MECA guideline snippet says "students at Huawei ICT academies or institutions willing to become one."

## 5. Does "Azure now, Huawei after qualifying" cost points?

Short answer: **yes, and probably more than you think.**

1. **Mandatory technology.** Every official Innovation document I found says participants "**must use** Huawei AI-related technologies (MindSpore, CANN, ModelArts)." The scoring line for 25 points explicitly rewards using Huawei tech and "specifying which ones." An Azure-only entry risks both a zero on that line and a compliance question. (2023-24 brief, **Verified**. 2024-25 flyer says the same, **Verified**. 2025-26 text not retrievable.)
2. **The first judging is the design submission with a demo video,** not a later stage. If your demo and video show Azure OpenAI and Azure Blob, the judges see no Huawei tech in the very artifact you are judged on. "We will migrate later" is a promise, not a score. The architecture diagram must show Huawei technologies at submission time.
3. **Your credit premise looks wrong.** The 2023-24 schedule shows Huawei Cloud resources distributed to **approved teams after registration review,** before design submission. You likely do not need to pass a stage to get credits. Verify with the Egypt ICT Academies contact, but plan on getting vouchers early.
4. **Heavy migration after qualifying wastes your best weeks.** Between regional and global stages you re-submit an optimised design. A forced cloud migration then competes with polish.

## 6. Implications for our PRD

- Replace "build and demo everything on Azure" with **"Huawei-first for the competition path, Azure for dev."** The judged demo path runs on ModelArts MaaS and OBS (and Huawei compute if available).
- Re-label the migration step: it is a **December pre-submission task,** not a post-qualification task.
- Add a **Huawei technology map** section to the PRD and to the design scheme document: which Huawei service provides which function, shown in the architecture diagram.
- Add acceptance criteria: demo runs end-to-end without Azure services, and an LLM-provider switch can be demonstrated live.
- Add a **team-eligibility check** milestone: three enrolled students from one university plus one instructor.
- Add a **"Huawei Cloud account and vouchers"** milestone this month: create the Huawei Cloud international account, ask the ICT Academy contact for vouchers, confirm region availability of MaaS.
- Stop calling the product a "GTM command center / revenue workspace" in the competition deck. Frame it around a problem the rubric values (see below).
- Use only **synthetic or consented demo data**. Enrichment and outreach connectors that touch real people's data collide with the "must not infringe on privacy" rule.

## 7. Pushback / risks

1. **Creativity is 30 points and "no well-known similar solution" is the test.** AI-assisted GTM and CRM tooling is a saturated category (Apollo, HubSpot AI, Clay, etc.). "Twenty plus an LLM" will read as a wrapper. Your defensible edge is **Arabic Saudi/Egyptian dialect generation for MENA solopreneurs** and anything you build that is genuinely new (dialect evaluation harness, MENA-specific playbooks). Lead with that, not with CRM.
2. **Social value is the weak spot.** Past winners are health, accessibility and heritage. The rubric does accept "commercial profitability," but an unframed B2B sales tool will score lowest here. Fix it with a credible story: Egyptian and Gulf micro-entrepreneurs who cannot afford English-first tools, women-led small businesses, job creation. Back it with numbers or interviews, not adjectives.
3. **"Built on open-source Twenty CRM" invites an originality attack.** The rules require entries to be "innovative, practical, and originally designed by teams." Be explicit about what your team built versus what Twenty provides, and show your own code in the diagram.
4. **A polished demo helps, but the rubric rewards Huawei tech and function over visual polish.** "Organizers like things that are ready" is consistent with the "fully functional, no glaring issues" rule. It does not replace Huawei integration.
5. **Dependence on rules I could not read.** 2026-27 specifics (topics, whether ModelArts is mandatory, weights, team photo requirements, exact Egypt dates) are unconfirmed. Ask the Huawei Egypt Talent Manager. Do not plan the whole build on a 2023-24 PDF.
6. **LLM availability.** MaaS regional availability near Egypt is unverified, and the Arabic Pangu model may not be accessible to you. Have a plan B (Qwen or DeepSeek on MaaS in the Singapore region) and measure the dialect quality honestly.
7. **Privacy and legal exposure.** Lead enrichment, outreach and scraping connectors can breach the "privacy" rule and local data laws (Saudi PDPL, Egypt PDPL). Disable or mock them in the competition build.
8. **Scheduling.** If the Egypt design submission is January to early February, a "December demo" target is fine, but you also need time for the application form, design scheme document, PPT and a video of 3 to 5 minutes. Budget for those documents separately from product work.

## 8. Practical pitch advice

**Proposal / design scheme**
- Open with a concrete problem and a person (for example, a Cairo freelancer or a Riyadh home-business owner and what they lose today).
- Include the architecture diagram with Huawei services highlighted. Name each one (ModelArts MaaS model, OBS, ECS/CCE, RDS) and what it does.
- A short section on **what is new** versus existing CRM-AI tools. This is the 30-point line.
- A **value** section: time saved, cost saved, number of users reached, pilot results. Real users beat claims (Manetho had about 700 early users).
- Show completeness: list all functions, error handling, data flow, permissions.

**Demo video (about 5 min preliminary, 3 min or less for final)**
- One uninterrupted path from start to finish on live Huawei-hosted infrastructure. Show the console or architecture overlay briefly to prove Huawei components.
- Show Arabic dialect output side by side with a generic English-first tool.
- Add English captions (presentation language is English; English gets bonus points in the Global Final context).

**Live presentation (national/regional 10 + 5 min, final 15 + 5)**
- Rehearse the Q&A. 15 points ride on how well you answer, and judges include Huawei R&D staff who will probe the technology.
- Expect questions: why Huawei components, what your own code is versus Twenty, how you evaluate Arabic quality, data privacy.

**Common reasons projects lose (inference from the rubric and rules)**
1. Huawei technology not visible or not specified.
2. Idea is generic or already exists commercially.
3. Demo breaks or covers only a happy path (rule: "fully functional... no glaring issues").
4. No clear social or commercial value with evidence.
5. Team composition does not meet the rule (3 students plus 1 instructor, same institution).
6. Reused prior-contest entry, plagiarism, privacy-violating content.
7. Overrunning time or weak Q&A.
