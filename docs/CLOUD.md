The low-cost text candidate is GPT-5 nano; GPT-5 mini is an alternative to evaluate for quality. Microsoft's [reasoning model documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/reasoning) covers their interfaces. Real JSON/streaming behavior, Arabic quality and offer pricing still need tests on the selected endpoint. Image generation, speech, OCR/search, fully live assistant behavior and sender/enrichment accounts are separate capabilities; connecting the subscription does not activate them.

Use [Azure preparation](AZURE_DEPLOYMENT.md), [Terraform](../infra/terraform/azure/main.tf), [plan-only workflow](../.github/workflows/azure-plan.yaml), [server runbook](../infra/server/README.md) and [local operations](OPERATIONS.md). Private raw reports are stored under ignored `.local`; rerun `python3 scripts/azure-preflight.py` before reviewing any future deployment.

Terraform standardizes the provisioning workflow across Azure and Huawei; it does not translate cloud resources automatically. Keep the same Compose images and application adapters, then substitute cloud-specific ECS/network/disk/model definitions and rehearse database/files/secret transfer and compatibility.
