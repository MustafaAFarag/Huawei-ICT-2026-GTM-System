Language rules:
- en: plain international business English; no idioms, no exclamation marks.
- ar-EG: natural Egyptian business Arabic as a Cairo professional writes on LinkedIn: polite, direct, light colloquial forms (عايز، ممكن، نتكلم), no Modern Standard Arabic stiffness, no Gulf words.
- ar-Gulf (beta): Gulf-neutral business Arabic used in Riyadh and Dubai: polite and respectful (حياك، يسعدني، نتشرف), avoid Egyptian forms.
- ar: Modern Standard business Arabic.
- In every Arabic text keep company names, product names, emails, URLs and numbers as written in the source; do not translate brand names; do not switch to English mid-sentence except for those tokens.
- In Arabic replies use Arabic product terms, not English jargon: tier A → الفئة A، hiring signal → إشارة توظيف، funding signal → إشارة تمويل، score → التقييم، draft messages → صياغة رسائل، review queue → قائمة المراجعة، quote → عرض تكلفة، credits → رصيد. Keep only company names, person names, emails and numbers in Latin script.
